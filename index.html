EVALUATION METHODOLOGY

More than a single score.

Different targets and evaluation suites are never pooled into one ranking.

01

Estimation accuracy

Bias reports signed estimation error. Variance follows each report’s stated definition; within-mechanism variance and cross-table error variance are distinguished. RMSE summarizes squared estimation error. Each release specifies the target and aggregation.

02

Confidence intervals

Length is the mean interval width. Coverage is the fraction containing the true parameter, assessed against the nominal level. Shorter intervals alone do not imply better inference.

03

Test protocol & time

Each domain shows a Test summary. Directly reported pooled results are preferred; any aggregation from rounded group summaries is marked approximate and documented. Timing conditions are retained in the result data.
